2. Assemble separated shaft bearings, shoulder, spacers/shims and DSH-10 retaining ring. Verify positive axial retention and free rotation by a future unpowered approved inspection.

6. Attach disc, caliper and guides; set0.65/0.35mm modeled pad clearances. Install qualified linings and actual return springs; wire/solid height not in current envelope model.

8. Fit guarded cover and encoder envelope support. Wire routes, magnet alignment and service access must be reviewed against selected encoder and actual harness.
