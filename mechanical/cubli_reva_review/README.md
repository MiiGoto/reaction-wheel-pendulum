# Pre-detail-CAD review only

`feasibility.json` is reproducible with `simulation/cubli_reva_gate.py` and
contains assumed size options, allocation rank/condition, brake force and
finite-time fixed-edge calculations. `native_readonly_check.json` records a real
Inventor reopening of the retained clearance assembly without saving it.

These are not a mechanical BOM or release mass-property handoff. No new
manufacturing .ipt/.iam/.idw/STEP/STL/DXF/PDF has been generated. The earlier
concept STEP/native study remains retained and labelled separately.

Open completion items: selected motor/order option, driver-current mapping and
loaded speed, encoder/magnet interfaces, separate wheel shaft/bearings, positive
retention, bidirectional brake/actuator force/stroke/duty, contact/catch dynamics,
impact-rated frame/guard, purchased power pack/absorber, precise PCB connector
envelopes, cable/fastener access, actual material density and finished mass,
rotor joints and full motion/interference, drawings, BOM and exports.
